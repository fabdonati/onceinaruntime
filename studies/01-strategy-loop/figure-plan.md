# Figures in the strategy article

The [figure index](figures/README.md) lists the six figures in reading order
and links to their data. The price and account charts use selected QQQ histories.
The timing charts use the historical main run and keep the original synthetic
campaign separate.

The traversal diagram shows the order of updates. The account-file illustration
shows the instructions for steady cash and long positions. The byte labels there
describe explicit account reads and writes. See the
[mechanism report](mechanism-check.md) for the instruction paths.

Follow the [benchmark guide](benchmark.md) to regenerate the historical plots.
The figure generators use the [publication palette and type settings](../../publication/visual-style.json).
